How it works

From three inputs to an evacuation plan

The simulator turns each scenario into village-level flood depth, then routes people from flooded villages to the nearest cyclone shelter.

Storm surge

Sea-level rise at landfall, up to 6 m. The main driver of how far water pushes inland.

Rainfall

Rain intensity adds to flooding, especially in low-lying delta villages.

Embankment strength

A stronger wall reduces flood depth. Below 40% the embankment is treated as breached.

Village risk

Each village has its own lowness factor. Depth of 2 m or more counts as high risk.

Evacuation routes

Flooded villages are linked to the nearest multipurpose cyclone shelter with an animated route.

Caveat

Depths and locations are illustrative. A real deployment needs elevation models, surveyed villages and tide gauge data.
